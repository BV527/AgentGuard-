### Battery Technologies
1. Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP): 3000+ cycles, thermal stability ~270C
2. NMC 811: 250-300 Wh/kg energy density
3. Solid-State: 400+ Wh/kg, non-flammable, commercial by 2027

### Key Trends
- Silicon-anode integration: +20% volumetric capacity
- Fast-charging: >350 kW DC networks expanding
- Recycling: >95% material recovery via hydrometallurgy
